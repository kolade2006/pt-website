WHO WE ARE

Welcome to Praise Tabernacle

Praise Tabernacle is a parish of the Redeemed Christian Church of God located in Ogombo, Lagos, Nigeria.

We are a church community centred on the worship of God, prayer, the teaching of His Word and fellowship among believers.

Our desire is to provide a welcoming environment where people can come together in faith, grow spiritually and build a relationship with God.
